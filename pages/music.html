    listener log

    page intentions: my favorite songs, my love of little music players, and my white-knight urge to keep music free and self-hosted when i can. apple, spotify, and youtube links are here if you prefer streaming there, but this player should work too. lock your phone, skip around, no ads.

    07/24/2026: being introverted as fuck, i turned to music a lot growing up. songs have this weird way of decorating time, making ordinary moments feel memorable, and making you feel a little less alone.

    i've been loyal to the same youtube account ever since i started surfing the web, so it's become this accidental archive of who i used to be. lately, my algorithm has been resurfacing everything i listened to when i was 17 or 18.

    it's strange how music can collapse time. in the same way the smell of the soap your grandma used to use can suddenly bring her back for a split second, a single song can throw you back into a version of yourself you hadn't thought about in years. not just the memories, but the feelings too. for a few minutes, you're there again.

    i figured i'd start keeping a little music log here, partly to preserve those moments, and partly because maybe someone else will stumble across a song that ends up decorating their own time.

    07/24/2026: i made the browser tell ios what song is playing. before, safari only knew the page title, so the lock screen showed “music - lupe zambrano.” now the player sends the current song title, artist, and cover art whenever the track changes.

    06/11/2026: i caught myself following the sterile GUI path. so i added decorations.
